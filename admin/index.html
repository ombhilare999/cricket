Squad quota

Full members can play up to 16 matches and half members up to 8, out of 24 across both tournaments. The tick on each bar shows where a player should be by now. These counts cover the whole season, whichever tournament is selected.

Batting

Opp is the number of times a player batted. Opp % is their share of matches played in which they got to bat. Click any column to sort.

Bowling

Opp is the number of matches a player bowled in. Balls/M is the average number of balls bowled per match played.

Workload

Balls faced (left) and balls bowled (right) per match played. A fair split means everyone gets a similar amount of cricket for their fee.
